# Final Project Status Report

## A. Project
**Title:** Audio Deepfake & Synthetic Voice Detector
**Objective:** To develop a robust, deep-learning-based classification system capable of analyzing speech recordings and identifying whether the audio is bona fide human speech or synthetically generated (spoofed).

## B. Final Architecture
Audio Upload (WAV/FLAC/MP3) → Decoded & Trimmed/Padded to 4s → Log-Mel Spectrogram Extraction (128x251) → Custom PyTorch CNN (AudioDeepfakeCNN) → Sigmoid Prediction → Grad-CAM Attention Extraction → Streamlit UI Rendering.

## C. Dataset
**Development/Training Dataset:** ASVspoof 2019 Logical Access (LA). Used extensively for model training, tuning, and threshold selection.
**Final Held-Out Evaluation Dataset:** ASVspoof 2021 Deepfake (DF). Used strictly for zero-shot testing; entirely unseen during training and parameter optimization.

## D. Final Model
**Architecture:** AudioDeepfakeCNN (custom Conv2D with BatchNorm, ReLU, Dropout, and Global Average Pooling).
**Frozen Checkpoint:** models/cnn_real/best_cnn_real.pth

## E. Final Threshold
**Threshold:** 0.5 (Fixed and untuned on the evaluation set).

## F. Final Held-Out Evaluation
The evaluation was executed cleanly against the ASVspoof 2021 DF dataset.
- Total files available: 60,176
- Successfully decoded/scored: 34,481
- Excluded/undecodable (codec failures in dataset): 25,695

Metrics (computed exclusively on the 34,481 valid samples):
- Accuracy: 0.4367
- Balanced Accuracy: 0.7007
- Precision: 0.9986
- Recall: 0.4188
- F1 Score: 0.5901
- ROC-AUC: 0.7920
- EER: 0.2839

## G. Main Limitation
The final held-out evaluation demonstrated strong precision but substantially lower recall on the 2021 dataset compared to the 2019 development set. This clearly indicates a **domain-shift and generalization limitation**. The model performs well on architectures similar to its training data but struggles to confidently flag novel, highly sophisticated vocoder/generation algorithms present in the later 2021 attacks.

## H. Explainability
Grad-CAM was successfully implemented. By extracting gradients from the final convolutional layer (`model.block4[0]`), the system accurately highlights specific time-frequency structures on the Log-Mel Spectrogram that influence the classification decision.

## I. Application
A professional, academic Streamlit application (`app/app.py`) was successfully built. It acts purely as a non-mutating inference frontend, exposing the preprocessor, CNN predictor, visualization engines, and historic evaluation metrics without altering underlying model structures.

## J. Testing

**Result:** 135/135 tests PASSED.
